BreezeSprint26 — Breeze-ASR-26 on Colab TPU

Turn Taiwanese Hokkien (Taigi) recordings into timestamped Chinese text.

Two cells: prepare, then upload. Completed recordings automatically download as timestamped TXT.

Experimental Breeze port: not yet physically TPU-tested by the packager.

ASR-26 renders Taigi speech in Mandarin Chinese characters. It does not promise native Taigi orthography, Tâi-lô, or phonetic verbatim transcription. It is not a universal upgrade over ASR-25 for Mandarin.

Open in Colab · Source and validation

Independent integration. Application MIT; model weights Apache-2.0.
